# Project 1 – The Cost of Living Crisis

## Research Question

How has inflation affected the prices of everyday goods in the United States between 2010 and 2025?

---

## Why This Project?

Inflation affects nearly every person by changing the prices of food, housing, transportation, and other necessities. Following the COVID-19 pandemic, inflation reached levels not seen in decades. This project analyzes official government data to understand how prices changed over time and what those changes mean for consumers.
